name: exclusivity
description: Exclusivity review procedures examine exclusive rights, sole suppliers, distribution arrangements.
---

## Goal

Determine which commercial dealings or rights are exclusive.

## Detection cues

Locate exclusive rights, sole supplier and exclusivity arrangement wording.

## Review steps

1. Identify parties, products, channels and territory.
2. Record duration, minimum commitments and performance conditions.
3. Check exceptions and exit rights for underperformance.

## Risk signals

Flag broad exclusivity without performance conditions or an effective exit.

## Required evidence

Quote the exclusive grant or restriction with its commercial boundaries.

## Recommendation rules

Recommend measurable conditions or narrower scope when the source exposes an imbalance.

## Failure and uncertainty

Do not equate a non-compete with exclusive supply; an undefined product scope requires an uncertain finding.
