name: confidentiality
description: Confidentiality procedures examine obligations, confidential information, nondisclosure duties, disclosure exceptions.
---

## Goal

Determine permitted uses and disclosures of protected information.

## Detection cues

Locate confidential information, non-disclosure and confidentiality obligations wording.

## Review steps

1. Identify protected information, parties and permitted uses.
2. Check public-domain, prior-knowledge and required-disclosure exceptions.
3. Record duration, survival, return duties and residual-knowledge permissions.

## Risk signals

Flag missing exceptions or an unclear survival period; distinguish trade secrets from other information when the text does.

## Required evidence

Quote the use or disclosure restriction with its exceptions and duration.

## Recommendation rules

Recommend clearer exceptions or duration only when the quoted duties need them.

## Failure and uncertainty

Do not infer confidentiality from a document label alone; unavailable exceptions require an uncertain finding.
