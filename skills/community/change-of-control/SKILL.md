## Goal

Identify rights and duties triggered by a control change.

## Detection cues

Locate change of control, controlling interest, merger or acquisition wording.

## Review steps

1. Identify the control threshold and relevant transaction.
2. Record termination, consent or notice consequences.
3. Read assignment rules and any affiliate or reorganization exceptions.

## Risk signals

Flag undefined thresholds, automatic termination or consent requirements without a response deadline.

## Required evidence

Quote the control trigger and resulting right or obligation.

## Recommendation rules

Recommend clear thresholds or consent mechanics where the quoted trigger is ambiguous.

## Failure and uncertainty

Do not infer a control-change clause from ordinary assignment language alone.
