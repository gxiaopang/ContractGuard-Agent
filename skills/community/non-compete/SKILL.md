## Goal

Identify restrictions on competing commercial activities.

## Detection cues

Locate non-compete, shall not compete and competitive business wording.

## Review steps

1. Identify the restricted party and activities.
2. Record territory, duration and post-termination survival.
3. Read exceptions and distinguish competition limits from non-solicitation.

## Risk signals

Flag broad activity definitions or open-ended restrictions; do not assert jurisdiction-specific enforceability.

## Required evidence

Quote the prohibited conduct together with its territorial and temporal limits.

## Recommendation rules

Recommend narrower commercial scope or specialist legal review when restrictions are unclear.

## Failure and uncertainty

Do not treat a confidentiality duty as a non-compete; incomplete boundaries require an uncertain finding.
