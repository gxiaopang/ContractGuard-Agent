---
name: cap-on-liability
description: Cap on liability procedures examine aggregate ceilings, monetary limits, fee multiples, sublimits.
---

## Goal

Identify the maximum monetary exposure and its coverage.

## Detection cues

Locate aggregate liability, shall not exceed, maximum exposure and fee-multiple wording.

## Review steps

1. Record the amount or formula and its calculation period.
2. Identify parties, claims and remedies covered by the ceiling.
3. Read carve-outs, separate sublimits and exclusions alongside the cap.

## Risk signals

Flag undefined fee periods, asymmetric ceilings or exceptions that remove major protections.

## Required evidence

Quote the cap formula with its scope and exceptions.

## Recommendation rules

Recommend clarification of the formula or coverage when evidence shows an ambiguity.

## Failure and uncertainty

Do not convert a missing cap into a detected cap; separate uncertain coverage from the stated formula.
