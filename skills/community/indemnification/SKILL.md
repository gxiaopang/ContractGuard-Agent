name: indemnification
description: Indemnification review procedures examine indemnity coverage, defense duties, covered claims, beneficiaries.
---

## Goal

Determine which claims and losses one party must cover for another.

## Detection cues

Locate indemnify, defend and hold harmless wording.

## Review steps

1. Identify indemnifying party, beneficiaries and covered claims.
2. Check notice, defense control, settlement consent and cooperation.
3. Read exclusions and liability caps that apply to the indemnity.

## Risk signals

Flag unrestricted loss coverage or defense duties without control over settlement.

## Required evidence

Quote the indemnity scope plus its procedural conditions and applicable limits.

## Recommendation rules

Recommend aligned defense control, notice or limits when the text exposes a specific gap.

## Failure and uncertainty

Do not treat every damages obligation as indemnity; unclear cross-references require more source text.
