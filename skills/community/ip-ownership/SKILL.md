name: ip-ownership
description: IP ownership assignment review procedures examine intellectual property transfers, background rights, retained licenses.
---

## Goal

Determine ownership of deliverables and retained intellectual property.

## Detection cues

Locate hereby assigns, work made for hire, ownership of deliverables and intellectual property wording.

## Review steps

1. Identify assignor, assignee and the covered work.
2. Separate pre-existing material from newly created deliverables.
3. Check license-backs, third-party material and timing or payment conditions.

## Risk signals

Flag transfers of background rights or a missing license needed to use retained components.

## Required evidence

Quote the assignment and any background-rights or license exceptions.

## Recommendation rules

Recommend explicit ownership boundaries and necessary licenses only where the text supports a gap.

## Failure and uncertainty

Do not equate a license with ownership; undefined deliverables require an uncertainty statement.
